# Changes — 12-04-26-f (Phase 4b Session 4 — Bitfield Settings + Full QMK Catalog)

## QMK Settings: complete catalog, bitfield expansion, width-aware protocol

Cross-referenced vial-qmk `qmk_settings.c` to get authoritative QSID→field mappings. Discovered our QSID constants were correct, but 3 bitfield QSIDs (0x01, 0x03, 0x15) were showing as "Unknown" with packed integer values. Also added width-aware protocol throughout, fixed slider init clobbering, and expanded catalog from 1 to 22+ settings.
